Import your music

In the music library, tap + and choose files to import. Aurody copies readable audio into its own storage. Files stored with a cloud provider may need to download first. Keep the original files: importing uses additional space.

Share from another app

Select an audio file in another app and choose Aurody in the system share menu. If the file cannot be read, save a complete local copy and select it again. Finish any pending import inside Aurody.

Transfer from your computer

  1. Connect your phone and computer to a trusted local network where they can reach one another.
  2. Open Wi-Fi transfer in Aurody. Keep the app in the foreground.
  3. In your computer’s browser, type the temporary address displayed on the phone. Enter its six-digit code and select your files.
  4. Wait for the import result, retry any failed files, then close the transfer session on your phone.

The transfer address comes from your phone, not this website. Local HTTP transfers are not encrypted. Use a trusted network; reopening transfer creates a new address or code. Codes expire after 15 minutes and access is blocked after five failed attempts.

If the address will not open, check the current address, local-network permission, firewall and guest-network isolation. A network change, backgrounding or locking the phone can interrupt transfer. Reopen the session and use the newly displayed details.

Audio and lyrics

Aurody handles common audio files such as MP3, AAC/M4A, FLAC, ALAC and WAV, subject to the actual codec and system support. A matching extension does not guarantee playback. Protected subscription downloads and DRM-encrypted files are not supported.

Use embedded lyrics or import a local LRC file. Valid timestamps allow synchronized lyrics. If automatic matching fails, associate the lyrics with a song manually. Missing lyrics do not stop music playback; lyrics are not fetched online automatically.

Offline and network audio

Music already copied into Aurody can play offline. Audio links you add yourself need a reachable source; temporary cached ranges are not a promise that the entire track will be available offline. Aurody does not supply an online music catalog.

Understand removal and deletion

  • Remove from a playlist: changes that playlist only; the audio file stays.
  • Remove from the music library: retains the internal audio; use file management to add a retained file back.
  • Delete an internal file: deletes that copy after confirmation and affects its library, favorite, playlist and queue references. The original file outside Aurody is unaffected.

Keep your originals

Uninstalling the app may remove its music, lyrics, playlists and other local data. There is no built-in cross-device sync or manual backup/restore feature. System backup or device migration may include app data depending on your operating system and settings; do not rely on it as your only copy.
