Aurody Privacy Policy

Information collection, use, storage and your choices.

Last updated: November 5, 2026

This policy describes the Aurody music app for Android and iPhone and its official website. Aurody is maintained by its developer, reachable at deqiutseng@gmail.com. The app plays music you provide; its core features do not require an account.

1. Information Collection

1.1 Files and information you provide

When you import a file, Aurody reads the selected audio or lyrics, copies the file into app storage, and processes information such as its filename, title, artist, album, artwork, duration and content fingerprint to organize the library and detect duplicates. Library records, playlists, favorites, queue and playback position are stored locally. Language settings and temporary media or artwork caches are also kept on the device.

1.2 Network requests you initiate

Wi-Fi transfer receives the files you select from a browser on your local network and uses a temporary code to authorize uploads. Transfer uses local HTTP, not encryption; use a trusted network and close the session when finished. When you add and play an HTTP/HTTPS audio link, the source server receives requests, including your IP address and requested URL, and may apply its own logging and privacy practices. HTTP audio traffic is not encrypted. Aurody may cache parts of the response locally. Selecting a cloud-hosted file may also contact your chosen file provider.

1.3 File access and system permissions

The system file picker and share menu let you choose which files to provide. Local-network access is used for Wi-Fi transfer where required by the system. Background playback and system media controls can expose the current song title, artist and artwork on the lock screen, notification area or connected media device. Aurody’s current music features do not request contacts, microphone or location access. Manage app permissions in your system settings.

1.4 Website preferences and hosting

This website has no analytics, advertising, subscription forms or third-party fonts. It stores only your manually chosen website language in this browser’s local storage; that preference is not uploaded by our site scripts. Clear the site’s browser data to remove it. The website is hosted on GitHub Pages: GitHub receives the network requests needed to deliver the pages and assets, and handles hosting information under its own privacy statement.

2. How We Use Your Information

Aurody processes your selected files and song information to import music, identify duplicates, organize and search the library, play audio and display lyrics. Preferences and playback position are used to restore your settings and listening context. Information you send by email is used to respond to your question.

3. Shared and Public Information

Aurody does not provide public profiles, a community feed or public music-library sharing. While you play music, the system may show the song title, artist and artwork on the lock screen, in notifications or on connected media devices. Wi-Fi transfer accepts files from the browser you connect to your phone.

4. Information Sharing and Disclosure

When you choose an external audio URL or cloud file, the relevant source or file provider handles the requests. System backup is handled by your platform provider; website delivery is handled by GitHub Pages. Sending email provides its content to the developer and your email provider. These services have their own policies. The reviewed Aurody app does not integrate advertising, analytics or crash-reporting services.

If you send an email, the developer receives your email address and the content or attachments you choose to send, to handle your question. Your email provider also processes the message under its own policies. Do not send music files or sensitive information unless needed to explain the issue. To ask about information in a support email, contact the same address.

5. Data Security

App files and records use the storage facilities provided by the operating system. Wi-Fi transfer uses a temporary six-digit code with an expiry and failed-attempt limit. Local HTTP transfer and HTTP audio links are not encrypted, so use a trusted network and close transfer after use. No method of storage or transmission is an absolute security guarantee; keep original music files and do not share transfer codes or private URLs.

6. Data Storage

Local storage does not mean that data can never leave the device. The current Android configuration allows system backup; iOS app data is not explicitly excluded from system backups. Depending on your device settings and system limits, app data may participate in cloud backup or migration handled by your platform provider. This differs from Aurody uploading your music to a developer-operated service. Keep your originals and manage backups through system settings.

You can remove library entries, delete internal files, clear network caches when allowed, change permissions, stop transfers and clear website preferences. Files and library data remain until removed by you, the app’s file-management actions or the operating system; temporary caches may be evicted earlier. Removing the app may delete its local data. Copies in system backups or at services you contact are managed separately by those providers.

7. Your Rights and Choices

You can view and manage the files and library records on your device, remove songs from playlists, delete internal audio copies, clear caches when allowed and change system permissions. Stop a Wi-Fi transfer to close its listening session. Clear this website’s browser data to remove its language preference. If you have questions about information you sent in a support email, contact the developer at the address below. Core Aurody features do not require an account, so there is no account-deletion procedure.

8. Children’s Privacy

If you are a minor, review this policy and manage the files you provide with a parent or guardian. A parent or guardian with a concern about information provided to the developer can contact the email address below. Please avoid sending unnecessary information about children in support emails.

9. International Data Transfers

Music and library data are stored on your device by default. Audio sources, cloud file providers, system backup providers, GitHub Pages and email services you choose may process requests in other regions under their own policies. Aurody does not promise that those services keep every request in your country or region.
